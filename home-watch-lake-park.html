Peace of Mind for Lake Park Homeowners

Lake Park sits southwest of Detroit Lakes, an easy drive to some of the area's finest water and a quiet home base for seasonal owners and year-round residents alike. When the weather turns and the house is left empty, a lot can go wrong between visits — frozen lines, a failed furnace, storm damage no one sees until spring.

That's what Sunbehm prevents. We make scheduled visits to your Lake Park-area property, inspect it inside and out, and send a photo report to your inbox every time — steady, documented care that means you never have to wonder whether your home is safe while you're gone.
