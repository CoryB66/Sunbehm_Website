The Basics

What Home Watch Is

What is home watch?

A scheduled, documented inspection of your home while you're away. We check inside and out for the problems that start small and get expensive — water, heat, pests, storm damage, security — and send a photo report after every visit.

How often do you visit?

Monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly, depending on the plan you choose. Many insurance policies expect an unoccupied home to be checked on a regular schedule, so it's worth confirming what your carrier requires.

What's in the visit report?

Photos and a written summary of what we found, in your email the same day. If something needs attention you'll know right away — not in the spring when the damage is done.

Trust & Coverage

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Sunbehm is insured, bonded, and locally owned — owner-operated by Cory Behm, not a franchise or a call center.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and location of your home and how often you'd like it visited. Estimates are free and there's no pressure — see the plans or ask for a quote.

What happens if you find a problem?

You hear from us right away, with photos. From there we can coordinate whatever comes next — meeting a plumber, electrician, or contractor on site so you're not managing a repair from three states away.
