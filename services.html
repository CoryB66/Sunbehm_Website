What We Do

Care for Homes of Every Kind

Sunbehm looks after lake homes, cabins, townhomes, condos, and the homes of year-round residents across Becker and Otter Tail County. Whether you're south for the winter, away for the season, or simply need a trusted local set of eyes, every visit is careful, unhurried, and documented.

Home watch is the foundation. Concierge help is added on as you need it — there's no package you have to buy your way into.

Home Watch Visits

Watch Plans

Every plan includes a full interior and exterior inspection and a photo-documented report sent to your inbox. Pricing depends on your home and location — ask for a free estimate.

Essential Watch

Monthly visits. One thorough home watch visit each month, full interior & exterior inspection, photo-documented visit report, and concierge services on request.

Preferred Watch

Bi-weekly visits. Two visits each month for closer oversight and added peace of mind, with full inspection, photo report, and priority scheduling.

Signature Watch

Weekly visits. Maximum protection and responsiveness, with full inspection, photo report, first-priority scheduling, and add-ons or concierge available.
