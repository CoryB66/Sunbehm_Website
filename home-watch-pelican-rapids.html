Between the Lakes

Peace of Mind for Pelican Rapids Homeowners

The Pelican Rapids area is ringed by some of the region's most sought-after water — Pelican Lake and the surrounding Otter Tail lakes among them. Many of the homes here are second homes and seasonal retreats, empty for months at a stretch while their owners are away. An empty house through a Minnesota winter is exactly where small problems quietly become expensive ones.

Sunbehm keeps that from happening. We visit on a schedule that fits your property, inspect it thoroughly inside and out, and send a photo-documented report every time — so you always know your Pelican Rapids home is safe, dry, and cared for, without having to drive up to check yourself.
