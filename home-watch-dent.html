Otter Tail County

Peace of Mind for Dent Homeowners

Dent sits in Otter Tail County at the eastern edge of our service area, surrounded by classic Minnesota lake country. Many of the properties here are summer cabins and seasonal lake homes — treasured in the warm months, then left quiet and empty when the snow flies and their owners head elsewhere for the winter.

Sunbehm keeps a careful watch in between. We visit your Dent-area property on a schedule that suits it, inspect it thoroughly inside and out, and send a photo-documented report every time — so a small issue is caught early, not discovered in the spring, and your lake home is always safe and ready for you.
