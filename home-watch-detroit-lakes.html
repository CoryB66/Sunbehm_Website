Peace of Mind for Detroit Lakes Homeowners

Detroit Lakes is the heart of Becker County's lake country — and when the season ends and you head south or back to the Cities, your home shouldn't be left to chance. Sunbehm Home Watch & Concierge is locally owned and based just up the road in Frazee, so we're never far when your property needs a trained eye.

Every visit is a thorough interior and exterior inspection — checking for leaks, heat, humidity, pests, storm damage, and signs of intrusion — followed by a photo-documented report sent straight to your inbox. If something needs attention, you hear about it right away, and we help coordinate whatever comes next.
