On the Cormorant Chain

Peace of Mind for Cormorant Homeowners

The Cormorant area sits on some of Becker County's most prized shoreline, strung between Big Cormorant and Little Cormorant Lakes just south of Detroit Lakes. Many of the homes here are seasonal retreats and cherished second homes — the kind of places that deserve a careful eye when their owners are away for the winter.

Sunbehm keeps watch. We visit your Cormorant-area property on a schedule that fits, inspect it thoroughly inside and out, and send a photo-documented report every time — so problems get caught early and you always know your home is safe, whether you're across the state or across the country.
